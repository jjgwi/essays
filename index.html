Looking at the Universe

Essays from the viewpoint of limited observers

Why this website?

Physics seeks universal laws, yet every experiment is performed from a finite location. Every laboratory, telescope, detector, or observer has access to only part of the physical world.

This website presents two complementary lines of research. One investigates how limited, bounded observers obtain information about the physical world (what can be known). The other investigates how physical constraints determine which responses and distinctions the world itself can sustain (which distinctions nature is capable of preserving). The essays emphasize physical intuition rather than technical proofs.

If you are curious about the broader motivation behind these essays, you may also enjoy reading Why Understanding Matters. If you are interested in the technical notes, don't hesitate to contact me.
