Looking at the Universe

Essays from the viewpoint of limited observers

Why this website?

Physics seeks universal laws, yet every experiment is performed from a finite location. Every laboratory, telescope, detector, or observer has access to only part of the physical world.

This website presents two complementary lines of research. One investigates how bounded observers obtain information about the physical world. The other investigates how physical constraints determine which responses and distinctions the world itself can sustain.
