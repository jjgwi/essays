Physics from the Viewpoint of Bounded Observers

Essays on quantum observation, boundary response, and bounded observers

Why this website?

Physics seeks universal laws, yet every experiment is performed from a finite location. Every laboratory, telescope, detector, or observer has access to only part of the physical world.

This website presents two lines of research that investigate this idea from different directions: quantum observation and boundary response.

How does a bounded physical observer obtain information about a larger physical world?
